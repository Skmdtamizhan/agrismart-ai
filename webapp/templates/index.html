💡 Tips for First-Time Farmers

  • Soil Health Card: Always consider getting an official soil health card from your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK). It costs almost nothing!
  • Crop Rotation: Never cultivate the same cereal crop twice in a row; rotate with legumes (chickpea, moong) to naturally fix atmospheric nitrogen.
  • Seed Treatment: Treating seed with Trichoderma or bio-agents prevents 60% of seedling rot diseases.
  • Avoid Over-Urea: Excessive urea turns foliage lush green but makes plants vulnerable to stem breakage and sucking pests.

Spray Safety Guidelines
  • Wind Speed: Best between 5–12 km/h. If wind exceeds 15 km/h, spray droplets drift away and contaminate non-target areas.
  • Temperature & Sun: Avoid spraying at peak noon (>32°C). Early morning or late evening prevents rapid evaporation and leaf scorching.
  • Rain Forecast: Ensure at least 4–6 rain-free hours after chemical foliar application for proper systemic absorption.
Fungal Disease Alert Rules

When relative humidity stays above 80% for more than 48 hours accompanied by mild temperatures (20°C–28°C), fungal spores (Rust, Blast, Blight) germinate rapidly.

Preventive Action

Apply preventive bio-control (Trichoderma / Pseudomonas) or Mancozeb spray before symptoms turn visible.
